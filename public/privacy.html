Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AviMiles handles information when you connect and use the service.

Information we collect

AviMiles may store your Discord user ID, Roblox user ID, Roblox username, Roblox display name, flight participation records, and AviMiles balances and transaction history.

How we use information

This information is used to connect your Roblox and AviMiles accounts, identify you during supported flights, maintain airline specific miles balances, prevent duplicate rewards, and operate AviMiles features.

Roblox authorization

AviMiles uses Roblox authorization to identify your Roblox account and access basic profile information you approve. AviMiles does not receive or store your Roblox password.

Sharing and retention

Information is used to operate AviMiles and its integrations. We do not sell personal information. Information may be retained while needed to provide the service, maintain records, prevent abuse, and meet applicable requirements.

Your choices

You can unlink your Roblox account using the AviMiles Discord bot. For questions or requests concerning stored information, contact AviTech through its official support channels.

Changes

This policy may be updated as AviMiles changes. The latest version will be available on this page.
